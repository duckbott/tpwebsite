About

I’m Tim Pow, a composer and musician from Scotland. I’ve been writing music for more than two decades, with a diverse background that spans classical music, ambient soundscapes, and collaborations with musicians from many genres. I love creating ambient scores that deeply connect with audiences, enhancing the emotional core of a scene and immersing viewers or players in the narrative. In 2015, I released a solo album, which was a blend of modern classical and post rock. In 2023, I released a solo EP which was an ambient work. More recently, I have studied film scoring at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland. From intimate, cinematic themes to grandiose orchestral scores, I bring a versatile approach to every project. If you're looking for a composer who can create music that resonates with your audience on an emotional level, let’s connect.
