About Me

Hi! I’m Tim Pow, a composer and musician from Scotland. I’ve been writing music for more than two decades, with a background that spans classical music, ambient soundscapes, and collaborations with artists across genres.

I love crafting ambient scores that enhance the emotional depth of a scene and immerse audiences in the story. In 2015, I released a solo album blending modern classical and post-rock. In 2023, I followed it with an ambient EP. More recently, I studied film scoring at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.

Whether it’s cinematic minimalism or full orchestral emotion, I approach every project with passion and versatility.
